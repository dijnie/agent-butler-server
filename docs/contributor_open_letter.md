# An Open Letter to Developers

"The duck is the first to know the spring river is warm, just when the pufferfish is about to swim upstream!"

Dear friend, I am John, an ordinary Java programmer at an ordinary company. Today, with the utmost sincerity, I am sending this open letter to you, who love AI technology and innovation.

Half a year ago I saw many excellent projects, such as `Dify` and `Chat2DB`, AI-related projects, and I thought how nice it would be if I could take part in them. Sadly, "no way to serve the country, ten years of coding in vain".

In early 2025 I came across the Xia Ge team's video, and I was very curious how he did it. I wanted to replicate their backend service and build a low-cost civilian Jarvis. Unfortunately, what I have built so far is still only an "artificial idiot": low concurrency, no soul, slow responses, and many bugs.

The Xia Ge team is who we learn from, and I would love to have a Xiaozhi backend service as smart as the Xia Ge team's. But I can also understand Xia Ge's decision not to open-source it. "One flower blooming alone is not spring; a hundred flowers blooming together fill the garden with spring." In an age when AI blossoms everywhere, perhaps it will be realized in our generation, and with our own hands we can build a low-cost civilian Jarvis. I personally believe that what he can do, we can do too; it is only a matter of time. I call it "our journey to the West to fetch scriptures".

So what difficulties will we meet on this journey? I think no fewer than eighty-one trials. All kinds of demons will inevitably appear along the way, and of course there will also be immortals secretly helping us, and others joining the pilgrim party.

If you find the above funny, then I feel very lucky too. If I can make you laugh for five seconds out of your 30,000-plus days of life, I have still made a contribution to you.

Will the idea of a civilian low-cost Jarvis fail? I do not know, but in the lives of ordinary people like us, isn't that kind of failure very common?

One thing about the future is certain: someone will definitely fully replicate the Xia Ge team's features and realize a civilian low-cost Jarvis. Will this project be us?

Looking forward to moving forward with you and creating the future together.

John, 2025.3.11, Guangzhou

# Appendix: Development Contribution Guide
## Project goals

1. **A civilian low-cost Jarvis solution**    

2. **A solution for smart linkage with surrounding hardware**    

## Join us

We warmly welcome like-minded friends to join us and contribute to the project. You can see the features we plan to implement soon at [this link](https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3). The items in the feature list that have not yet been assigned to anyone are exactly where we urgently need your participation. The ways to participate are as follows:

### 1. Become a regular contributor

Fork the project and submit a PR; developers review it and merge it into the main branch.

### 2. Become a developer

After you have submitted 3 valid PRs in total, you can contact the group owner to apply to become a developer, and the group owner will invite you to a separate developer group to discuss the future of the project together.

## Developer workflow

1. **Create a new branch**    
   Develop each feature on a new branch. The branch name should be short and clear, so that the feature it implements is obvious at a glance and features do not collide.

2. **Submit a PR for review**    
   When a feature is complete, submit a PR on GitHub. Other developers review it, and after approval it is merged into the main branch.
