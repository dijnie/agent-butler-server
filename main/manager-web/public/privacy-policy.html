Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to the Xiaozhi backend service (hereinafter "the Service"). The operator of the Service is the party that actually deploys and manages it (hereinafter "the Operator" or "we"). We are well aware of how important personal information is to you and will do our utmost to keep your personal information secure.

Before using the Service, please read this Privacy Policy carefully and make sure you fully understand all of it. Once you start using the Service, you are deemed to have read and agreed to this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to our collection, storage, use, sharing and protection of your personal information when you use the Service through the control console (admin backend), the API, or by other means.

1. Information We Collect

To provide the Service to you, we may need to collect the following information:

1.1 Account registration information: When you register an account, we collect your mobile number or username, password and similar information to create and verify your account.

1.2 Device information: When you bind a hardware device, we collect the device's identifiers (such as device code and MAC address), device model, firmware version and so on, for device management and service delivery.

1.3 Agent configuration information: When you create and configure an agent, we collect the role template, language model selection, voice parameter configuration and other information you set, in order to provide a personalized AI interaction service.

1.4 Voice interaction data: When you use the voice interaction feature, the Service uses voice activity detection (VAD) to determine when your speech starts and ends, processes your voice input data, and transmits it to third-party speech recognition (ASR) and language model (LLM) services for processing in order to provide voice interaction.

1.5 Image data: When you use the vision model feature, we may process image data captured through your device's camera and transmit it to third-party vision model services for analysis and understanding, in order to provide image recognition, scene understanding and similar features.

1.6 Conversation memory data: When you enable the memory feature, the Service stores summaries of your interaction history with the agent, in order to provide a more coherent and personalized experience in later conversations.

1.7 Knowledge base data: When you use the knowledge base feature, we collect and store the documents, text and other knowledge base content you upload, so that the agent can perform knowledge retrieval and question answering in conversations.

1.8 Voiceprint data: When you use the voiceprint recognition feature, we collect and store your voiceprint feature samples for speaker identity verification and personalized service.

1.9 Chat history data: We store your conversation history with the agent, including text conversation content, conversation time, interaction context and similar information, in order to provide conversational continuity and history lookup.

1.10 Conversation audio data: When you use the voice interaction feature, we may store audio data of your interactions with the agent, in order to improve voice interaction quality and allow later review.

1.11 Agent configuration data: When you configure an agent, we collect the tags, plugin configuration, context provider settings and other information you set, in order to provide agent customization.

1.12 Device firmware data: When you use the OTA upgrade feature, we record device firmware versions, upgrade history and similar information for device management and firmware traceability.

1.13 Log information: When you use the Service, we automatically collect service log information, including but not limited to access time, IP address, browser type and operation records, for service operations and security.

1.14 Verification code information: When you log in with a mobile number, we send you a verification code through an SMS service for identity verification.

2. How We Use the Information We Collect

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

(1) To provide, maintain and improve the Service, including core features such as account management, device management and agent configuration.

(2) To process your voice interaction requests: detecting voice input through voice activity detection (VAD) and calling third-party AI model services to perform speech recognition, intent recognition, semantic understanding and speech synthesis.

(3) To process your image data by calling third-party vision model services to perform image recognition and scene understanding.

(4) To store and manage your conversation memory data so as to provide a more coherent experience in later interactions.

(5) To store and retrieve the knowledge base content you upload, so that the agent can give you more accurate knowledge-based answers in conversations.

(6) To ensure the security and stability of the Service, including identity verification, security protection and troubleshooting.

(7) To comply with applicable laws, regulations and regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal information for any purpose unrelated to those above. If we need to use the information for a purpose not stated in this Privacy Policy, we will obtain your consent in advance.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share or disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 Third-party service calls: To provide features such as voice interaction and visual understanding, your voice data, image data and text content may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers (such as language model, speech recognition, speech synthesis and vision model providers) for processing. We select providers with reasonable security safeguards, but please be aware that third-party providers handle such data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

3.2 Legal requirements: We may need to disclose your personal information as required by applicable laws and regulations, legal proceedings or competent government authorities.

3.3 Security: We may use or disclose personal information to the extent reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of the Operator, other users or the public from harm.

3.4 With consent: We may share your personal information with third parties with your explicit consent.

4. Storage and Protection of Information

4.1 Storage location: Your personal information is stored on the servers where the Operator deploys the Service, including but not limited to databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL) and cache services (Redis).

4.2 Retention period: We retain your personal information for the period necessary to provide the Service to you. After you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time, unless laws and regulations provide otherwise.

4.3 Security measures: We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal information, including but not limited to data encryption, access control and security auditing. Please understand, however, that the internet is not an absolutely secure environment, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmission and storage.

4.4 Security incident handling: If a personal information security incident occurs, we will, as required by laws and regulations, promptly inform you of the basic facts of the incident, its possible impact, and the measures we have taken or will take.

4.5 Special note on the open-source project: The Service is an open-source project and has two operating models:

(1) Self-deployment: If you deploy the Service yourself, the Operator is only the provider of the code, and you are responsible for the actual data storage and processing. In this case, you are both the user of the Service and the manager and protector of the data.

(2) Test platform: If you use the test platform deployed by the Operator, your data is managed and protected by the operations staff. The test platform is for trial and testing only; we may clean up data periodically, and we recommend that you do not upload sensitive personal information.

If you are using a service deployed by someone else, please be aware that your data is managed and protected by that deployer.

4.6 Cross-border data transfer: When providing intelligent interaction features, the Service may need to call the interfaces of third-party AI service providers located overseas. In the process, your voice data, text data, image data and so on may be transmitted to servers outside the People's Republic of China for processing. The overseas providers involved include but are not limited to:

(1) OpenAI: provides speech recognition (Whisper), speech synthesis (TTS), large language model (GPT) and other services.

(2) Anthropic: provides large language model (Claude) services.

(3) Groq: provides speech recognition (Whisper) services.

(4) Microsoft EdgeTTS: provides speech synthesis services.

In accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Measures for Security Assessment of Outbound Data Transfers and other laws and regulations, we will take the necessary measures to keep your personal information secure during cross-border transfer. The Operator should clearly state on the control console whether the third-party services in use involve cross-border data transfer, and disclose the name and contact details of the data recipient, the purpose and method of processing, the categories of personal information, and similar information. If you do not agree to your data being transferred overseas, you may choose to use only AI services from domestic providers (such as Zhipu AI, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu Wenxin and iFlytek) or local deployment options (such as FunASR, FishSpeech and Ollama).

7. Third-Party Services

7.1 When providing intelligent interaction features, the Service needs to call third-party services, including but not limited to:

(1) Voice activity detection service (VAD): detects when voice input starts and ends.

(2) Speech recognition service (ASR): converts your voice input into text.

(3) Large language model service (LLM): performs semantic understanding, intent recognition and reply generation on text.

(4) Speech synthesis service (TTS): converts text replies into voice output.

(5) Vision model service: recognizes and understands images.

(6) SMS verification code service: used for identity verification during user registration and login.

(7) MQTT message broker service (MQTT Broker): used for message communication between devices and the server, including delivering device control commands and reporting device status.

(8) Database service: used for persistent storage of user data, device data, agent configuration, chat history and other information.

7.2 The third-party service providers above process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We recommend that you learn about the privacy policies of the relevant third-party providers before using the Service. The Operator should publish information about the third-party services in use on the control console.

7.3 We make reasonable efforts to select third-party providers that are legally compliant and have adequate security safeguards, but we are not responsible for the third-party providers' own data processing.
