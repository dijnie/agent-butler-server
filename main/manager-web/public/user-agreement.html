4. Service Content

4.1 Service overview: Relying on artificial intelligence technology, the Service calls third-party generative AI models through APIs to provide intelligent interaction services for connected hardware devices, including but not limited to voice activity detection (VAD), speech recognition (ASR), semantic understanding (LLM), speech synthesis (TTS), visual understanding (VLLM), intent recognition (Intent), conversation memory (Memory) and knowledge base (RAG).

4.2 Agent management: You can create and configure agents through the control console, including setting role templates, selecting language models, configuring voice parameters, setting intent recognition rules, managing knowledge bases, enabling conversation memory and configuring agent plugins. Agent settings must not violate national laws and regulations.

4.3 Device management: You can bind and manage your hardware devices through the control console and perform device configuration, firmware updates, OTA remote upgrades and similar operations. You must ensure that the devices you manage are lawfully owned by you or that you are authorized to manage them.

4.4 Visual understanding: The Service supports capturing images through the device camera and calling third-party vision models for image recognition and scene understanding. You must ensure that capturing images complies with applicable laws and regulations and does not infringe the privacy of others.

4.5 Intent recognition: The Service can use intent recognition to understand the intent of user commands and trigger the corresponding operations or services accordingly, such as controlling smart devices or looking up information.

4.6 Conversation memory: The Service can record and store summaries of your interaction history with the agent in order to provide a more coherent and personalized experience in later conversations. You can manage or clear memory data in the control console.

4.7 Knowledge base: The Service lets you upload documents, text and other content to build a knowledge base, and the agent can retrieve knowledge base content in conversations to answer your questions. You must ensure that the knowledge base content you upload does not infringe third-party intellectual property rights and does not contain content that violates laws and regulations.

4.8 Voiceprint recognition: The Service supports voiceprint recognition. You can register voiceprint samples to enable speaker identity verification and personalized service. Voiceprint data is used for identity verification purposes.

4.9 Voice cloning: The Service supports voice cloning. You can clone a custom voice by providing audio samples. Cloned voices are for your own use only and must not be used to impersonate others or to engage in illegal or non-compliant activities.

4.10 MCP endpoint: The Service supports the MCP (Model Context Protocol) protocol. It can act as an MCP Server providing tool-calling capabilities externally, and can also act as an MCP Client calling external MCP services, enabling standardized integration with external systems.

4.11 Context provider: The Service supports the Context Provider feature, which can obtain real-time information such as weather, news and stocks from external data sources, giving agents richer sources of knowledge.

4.12 MQTT protocol: The Service supports the MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport) protocol for message communication with IoT devices. Through MQTT you can deliver device control commands, monitor device status and so on.

4.13 Service notes: The conversations and replies generated by the third-party AI models on which the Service relies are for reference only and do not constitute professional advice of any kind. You must not treat the output as professional advice in fields such as medicine, law or finance. You bear full responsibility for any judgment or decision you make based on the output.

4.14 Service fees: The Service is a free open-source project. It does not charge users any usage fee, only provides the platform capability to call third-party services, and does not involve any paid features or value-added services.

The Service supports multiple AI service providers. The charging models of different providers are as follows:

(1) Free services: Some providers offer a free quota or completely free services, such as Zhipu AI (free models such as glm-4-flash) and Microsoft EdgeTTS speech synthesis.

(2) Pay-as-you-go services: Some providers charge by API usage, such as OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud Intelligent Speech, Baidu Wenxin and iFlytek. You must pay the corresponding fees to the third-party providers yourself; those fees have nothing to do with the Service.

(3) Local deployment options: The Service supports fully locally deployed AI options, such as FunASR speech recognition, FishSpeech speech synthesis and Ollama local large models. Local deployment incurs no network or API usage fees but requires sufficient local computing resources.

You can choose a suitable service provider according to your actual needs and budget. For specific pricing, please refer to each provider's official documentation.
